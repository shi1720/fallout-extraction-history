# Source notes

Printed page numbers are retained because PDF page counts include covers and preliminary matter. In the pinned HASL-113 PDF, printed p. 99 is PDF page 108 (one-based).

## HASL-113

- pp. 2–3: 40 domestic and 86 foreign sites; 86/126 use funnel/column collectors; four sites operate both methods. The material does not establish the resin lot at each site. Starting with May–June 1960, monthly samples were combined at most sites. This changes the relation between a table row and a physical collector.
- p. 3: reported divergence beginning July 1960; unused-column checks; about 17 dpm per column, expressed as 0.24 mCi/mi²; retrospective subtraction per column; acid extraction for collections beginning March 1961; new resin obtained. No blanket correction is applied in this artifact.
- pp. 75–80, Long D. Y. Ong: measurement reproducibility differs from the Poisson counting error printed with an assay. The 180-pair dataset is described, but individual pair values are not reconstructed here.
- pp. 81–84, C. R. Wilson: counter calibration, replicate samples, and blanks test different parts of the process. The contractor's error statement is conditional and is not imported as the error distribution for Hardy's fusions.
- pp. 97–100, E. Hardy: account of earlier nine-soil comparison and the sixteen replacement comparisons; contaminated initial fusion setting; no reproducibility study for replacement run; additional blind tests being prepared at NBS. No eventual outcome is inferred.
- p. 99: rows transcribed from scan. Site 10 clearly reads 14, 21, 67. Summary clearly reads minimum 78. Preserve both.
- p. 98: printed SSE 359 and horizontal intercept somewhat above two. Reconstructed printed data give SSE 371.47 and x-intercept 2.5028. A rounding-compatible witness explains the former difference.

## HASL-105

- Front matter confirms report number, preparers, reporting period, and issue date (9 January 1961).
- pp. 2–3: HASL versus contractor analysis; local collectors; publication of revisions. Early 1960 values checked in this issue were not evidence of a simple uniform shift between editions, so no cross-edition subtraction claim is made.

## UNSCEAR 1962

Annex F, Part II, paragraphs 17–20 concerns extraction and availability in the soil–plant pathway. These are distinct from a total sampled-soil inventory. The manuscript uses it to delimit the historical object; it does not equate all acid protocols or infer dietary risk from the sixteen-soil dataset.

## Provenance rules

Reported and recalculated values remain separate. Identifiers are not assigned guessed locations. Missing replicates are not generated. Historical actors' causal attributions are described as their published explanation. An implemented correction, a planned experiment, and an independently verified chemical result are different evidential categories.
